# First Horizon Corporation (first-horizon-national)

First Horizon Corporation (NYSE: FHN), headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee, is a regional financial-services holding company operating across 12 southeastern U.S. states through its principal subsidiary, First Horizon Bank. The franchise reports roughly $84 billion in total assets, $67 billion in deposits, and approximately 410 banking centers as of Q1 2026.

Business lines include consumer and small-business banking, commercial and specialty lending, private banking and wealth management, and FHN Financial fixed-income capital markets. The company was founded in 1864 as First National Bank of Memphis, rebranded as First Tennessee in 1977, became First Horizon National Corporation in 2004, and shortened its name to First Horizon Corporation following the 2020 IberiaBank merger.

In February 2022 First Horizon announced a $13.4 billion all-cash acquisition by Toronto-Dominion Bank; the parties mutually terminated the merger on May 4, 2023, citing regulatory uncertainty around the deal's closing timeline, with TD paying a $225 million termination fee plus a previously agreed $25 million reimbursement to First Horizon. First Horizon has since pursued an independent strategy under CEO Bryan Jordan.

First Horizon does not publish a public developer API portal. Customer account data is reachable for fintech use cases only through US open-banking aggregators (Plaid, MX, Finicity, Akoya) and through emerging Financial Data Exchange (FDX) APIs that will be required of covered data providers under the CFPB Section 1033 Personal Financial Data Rights rule. Commercial APIs for treasury management, ACH origination, wire initiation, and host-to-host file transfer are available to corporate clients under direct contract through First Horizon Treasury Management Services but are not publicly documented.

## Merger & Acquisition History

### IberiaBank Corporation — Completed (July 2020)
All-stock merger of equals closed July 1, 2020. The combined entity rebranded as First Horizon Corporation with roughly $79 billion in pro forma assets at close, expanding the franchise across the Southeast and Gulf South.

### Toronto-Dominion Bank — Terminated (May 4, 2023)
- Announced February 28, 2022
- Structure: all-cash, $25.00 per share, total deal value $13.4 billion
- Terminated mutually on May 4, 2023
- TD paid First Horizon a $225 million termination fee, on top of $25 million previously agreed reimbursement
- Stated reason: regulatory uncertainty — TD could not predict when required U.S. and Canadian bank-regulatory approvals would be obtained, and the parties agreed the deal could not close in a commercially reasonable timeframe. The deal collapsed amid heightened regulator scrutiny of large bank M&A and TD's own AML compliance issues, which later resulted in a $3B+ U.S. AML settlement in 2024.

## APIs
